# Discrete crop progression scenarios

Generated by `npm run balance:progression` from the current game catalog. This complements the steady-state balance report; it is not observed player pacing.

## Assumptions and limits

- Starts at level 1 with 12 plots and 500 gold. Follows every level through 50, the highest crop/expansion unlock level; game levels continue beyond it.
- Visits occur at fixed intervals, including minute zero. Crops mature between visits but are harvested only on a visit. Each visit harvests and sells everything ready, then makes one bulk planting decision. Planting and harvest XP remain separate, carry across levels, and can unlock several levels on one visit.
- The xp policy maximizes the next affordable batch's sow-plus-harvest XP per visit cycle; gold maximizes its net proceeds. Both are greedy choices, not globally optimal strategies. Partial batches respect available cash and free plots; unready crops remain planted across unlocks.
- No expansions, skills, watering bonus, quests, orders, collection rewards, animals, trees, machines, gifts, theft, gem spending or manual action time. These are crop-only paths, not total-game forecasts or claims that every player stalls when a scenario stalls.
- Price 1 uses unsaturated base proceeds; price 0.4 holds the market floor throughout as a sensitivity bound. Neither simulates shared market demand or saturation decay. Each plot's four-item harvest is sold separately, matching per-sale integer rounding.
- Tax uses 2000 gold per plot from level 20. A new day charges once on the next visit and unpaid debt blocks planting, while harvest and sale can repay it. Days are fixed 24-hour intervals anchored at the initial visit, not calendar dates; daylight-saving transitions and a different first-visit offset are excluded.
- A scenario stops when it reaches the target, cannot plant with no crops left to sell, or hits 20,000 visits. Cash and cumulative spending/sales are recorded when each level is first observed after the visit's harvest/sale/tax settlement or bulk sowing. Several levels may share a timestamp.
- Do not tune prices from these scenarios alone. Compare slow levels, cash reserves and stalls with five observed sessions and deployed economy totals.
